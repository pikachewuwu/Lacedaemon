Protocol

Nothing is printed. Every unit is earned.

DAEMON runs on three parts. Players on the surface never need to see any of them. Dragons stay the everyday money; DAEMON is for those who want a stake in what gets built.

Ledger

Transfers

Final, atomic, peer to peer. Send in the open or hidden; hidden transfers log the amount and nothing else. A DAEMON/Dragon order book sets the price.

Convergence

The daily arena

Commit DAEMON to rock, paper or scissors. Commitments are hidden and final. The smallest pool wins half. The two largest fight for the other half.

Governance

Rules can change

A proposal sets its own approval threshold as a share of circulating supply. Holders vote with weight. Even the consensus mechanism can be replaced.

Supply

Toward 21,000,000

Emission starts at 7,200 a day and drops by a tenth every year, rounded down. Hitting a supply threshold can cut it early. The curve flattens into the cap and stops there.

Modules

Everything that pays out is a module.

The arena, game night and the Minecraft vault are all modules. Anyone can build a new one. Players just play it, mostly for Dragons, and never touch DAEMON. The builders and backers underneath earn DAEMON when people show up.

  1. 1

    Lock

    A builder locks DAEMON behind a module as a bond. Others can lock alongside to back it. Longer locks count for more.

  2. 2

    Play

    People play. The module reports activity: unique players, sessions, how often they come back.

  3. 3

    Weigh

    Once a day, the Arbiter scores every module on activity and conviction and decides the split.

  4. 4

    Emit

    That day's emission flows to each module, then to its builder and backers in proportion to what they locked.

The Arbiter

An open model divides the emission. Its weights, prompt and inputs are public, so anyone can run the same epoch and check they get the same answer. It judges two things: whether people actually play a module, and how much its backers are willing to lock behind it.

  • Open weights, pinned. One named model version, temperature 0, seeded by the epoch number.
  • Published before it pays. Prompt, inputs and output are hashed and posted. A 24-hour window to contest follows.
  • Bounded. No module gets more than half. Convergence never gets less than a tenth. Weights sum to one.
  • Never stuck. If the model fails or breaks a bound, a plain formula runs instead: √activity × √conviction.
  • Replaceable. Governance can veto an epoch or swap the model.

Governance

No consensus mechanism is eternal.

Anyone holding DAEMON can propose a change and name the share of supply needed to pass it. Bigger changes ask for more. Proposal slots are scarce, so you can bid DAEMON for the next one. The rules evolve only when the holders agree.
